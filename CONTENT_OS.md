# Digital Renaissance Content OS

## Pipeline

IDEA → RESEARCH → ARTICLE → QA → APPROVAL → ARCHIVE → DERIVATIVES → DISTRIBUTION → ANALYTICS

## Daily job
1. Select the next category using rotation plus recent-topic avoidance.
2. Generate a 500–800 word article unless the idea works better as a short hit.
3. Apply the Digital Renaissance voice rules.
4. Flag claims needing evidence.
5. Generate derivatives only after the main article survives editorial QA.
6. Store the canonical article and metadata.
7. Queue distribution assets for human approval where an external publisher connection isn't available.

## Derivatives
For each approved article, generate:
- 3 X posts
- 1 LinkedIn post
- 1 Instagram caption
- 1 quote card text
- 1 short-form video script
- 1 newsletter teaser

## Editorial QA
Reject or rewrite if:
- The hook is generic.
- The article sounds like corporate self-help.
- It pads the word count.
- The argument has no actual point.
- It repeats a recent article without adding a new angle.
- Sensitive factual claims are presented without support.
- Provocation exists only for shock value.

## Distribution rule
Substack publishing is not currently connected. The system therefore prepares publication-ready content and distribution queues rather than pretending it can publish to an account it cannot access.
